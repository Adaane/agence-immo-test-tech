# Maison Vérane

Vocabulary of the Maison Vérane showcase site: the luxury properties the agency sells in Paris and Lyon, and how they are presented to visitors.

## Language

**Bien**:
A property offered for sale by Maison Vérane, located by its city and district, never by its address.
_Avoid_: Annonce, listing, propriété

**Fiche**:
The page dedicated to a single Bien, reached from its card on the homepage.
_Avoid_: Page détail, page produit

**Visite immersive**:
A scroll-driven sequence of camera moves through the rooms of a Bien, shown at the top of its Fiche.
_Avoid_: Vidéo, tour virtuel, scroll video

**Exclusivité**:
A Bien that Maison Vérane alone is mandated to sell. Every Exclusivité, and only an Exclusivité, has a Visite immersive.
_Avoid_: Mandat exclusif, bien phare
